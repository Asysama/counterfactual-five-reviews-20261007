true 独立提示词

A rotating lead screw drives a threaded cutaway nut along a visible anti-rotation guide. A latch then locks the screw against rotation; the bearings hold it against axial motion. The nut advances while the screw turns, then stops and remains engaged on the locked screw.

counterfactual 独立提示词

A rotating lead screw drives a threaded cutaway nut along a visible anti-rotation guide. A latch then locks the screw against rotation; the bearings hold it against axial motion. The nut advances while the screw turns, but after the screw locks the still-engaged nut continues moving axially even though neither the screw nor the guided nut rotates.
